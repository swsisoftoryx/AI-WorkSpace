# Website and app launch checklist

Use this as a prompt/checklist when reviewing a website or app. It is
not a substitute for legal advice or a jurisdiction-specific compliance
review.

-   [ ] Add a privacy policy.
-   [ ] Add terms and conditions.
-   [ ] Add a cookies policy where applicable.
-   [ ] Check cookie consent and tracking behavior.
-   [ ] Add a refund policy where relevant.
-   [ ] Add consent language to forms where needed.
-   [ ] Collect only necessary data.
-   [ ] Review analytics and tracking.
-   [ ] Review third-party embeds.
-   [ ] Check accessibility.
-   [ ] Add meaningful alt text to images.
-   [ ] Check color contrast.
-   [ ] Ensure forms are keyboard accessible.
-   [ ] Use clear button labels.
-   [ ] Remove fake reviews.
-   [ ] Remove misleading or unsupported claims.
-   [ ] Add accurate business details.
-   [ ] Check image licenses and copyright.
-   [ ] Review applicable legal and regulatory obligations.
-   [ ] Flag any other potential risks.

# Starting a company

The original notes suggest this general sequence. Check each service's
current availability, pricing, and terms before relying on it.

1.  Find a business idea using [Startup.ai](https://startup.ai/) or
    another idea-research resource.
2.  Brainstorm a name with a business-name generator.
3.  Ask an adviser or AI assistant to help outline a business model.
4.  Research domain availability and register a suitable domain.
5.  Validate product ideas with customer research.
