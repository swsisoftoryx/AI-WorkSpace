> Mental models, discipline frameworks, philosophy, and daily challenges to build an unstoppable mind.

## 1. The 1-Week Discipline Challenge

Follow this for 7 days:

1. Make your bed every day.
2. Drink a full glass of water after waking up.
3. Take a 30-minute walk every single day.
4. Write down your top priority every morning — finish that list before anything else.
5. Do one thing every day that makes you uncomfortable.
6. Wake up at the exact same time every day.
7. Stop self-complaining — when you do, come up with a solution.
8. Finish everything you said you'd do in the day.
9. Say no to every unnecessary invitation, call, and distraction.
10. Exercise every day.

---

## 2. The 7-Day Reset Challenge

1. No phone for the first 30 minutes after waking or before sleeping — plan your day (order + priorities) instead.
2. Spend 30 minutes daily visualizing who you want to become.
3. Before sleeping, reflect on the day (postpartum/journal).

## 3. Non-Negotiable Daily Habits

- Eliminate sugar.
- 10 minutes of daily meditation.
- 5 liters of water.
- Cold water shower.
- No screens after 10 PM and before 8 AM.
- The 3-Hour Disconnect (at least 1 hour for a week).
- Journal the day.

## 4. The Discipline Equation

> **Discipline = (Importance of Goal × Psychological Enjoyment) − Psychological Cost**

Behavior occurs when: **Positive Outcome − Friction > 0**

Key notes:
- Focus on the pain and struggle that aligns with what you can accomplish.
- Health is the first priority for everything you love and care about.
- Discipline is the key to effective time management and life choices.
- Many people are driven by pain rather than pleasure.
- Deep shame and insecurity can drive obsessiveness.
