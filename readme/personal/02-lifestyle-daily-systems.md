## 1. Personal Finance Framework

### Step-by-Step Money Management

1. **Track expenses monthly** — split into fixed vs. fluctuating expenses.
2. **Budget rule (50/20/30):**
   - 50% → fixed expenses
   - 20% → variable expenses
   - 30% → savings
3. **Emergency fund** — keep a safety net in the bank; invest where you can access it when needed but not too easily.
4. **Insurance** — get health insurance with good coverage and access to good hospitals.
5. **Save for goals** — start early in your career.
6. **Pay off debt** — focus on clearing debts.
7. **Invest** — spread money across different baskets.
8. **Retirement plan** — plan your retirement fund.
9. **File ITR every year.**

## 4. Daily Productivity Techniques

### 4.1 The Pomodoro Technique
1. Set a timer for 25 minutes.
2. Work on one task only.
3. Keep your phone out of reach.
4. Take a 5-minute break (walk, stretch, hydrate).
5. After 4 cycles, take a 20–30 minute break.

### 4.2 The Feynman Technique
1. Choose a concept.
2. Explain it as if teaching a 12-year-old.
3. Avoid jargon.
4. Notice where you struggle — those are knowledge gaps.
5. Relearn those parts.
6. Explain again simply.

> If you can't explain it simply, you don't understand it well enough.

### 4.3 The 1% Rule
Improve one skill or habit by a tiny amount every day:
- Read 1 more page
- Take 1 more step
- Send 1 more outreach message
- Spend 5 more minutes learning

Track your progress — compound effect over months/years.

### 4.4 The 5-Second Rule
The moment you think about doing something productive:
**5 → 4 → 3 → 2 → 1 → MOVE**

Use it to:
- Get out of bed
- Make that difficult call
- Start a workout
- Begin an important task
- Start studying

> Don't wait until you feel motivated. Act first.

### 4.5 Make It a Daily System
- Pomodoro → Focus
- Feynman → Understand
- 1% Rule → Improve
- 5-Second Rule → Take Action

## 5. Study & Learning Systems (for Daily Life)

### Immersion Method
Pick one subject per day and study it deeply — watch videos, take notes, solve questions.

### The 80/20 Study Rule
Focus on the 20% of topics that bring 80% of marks.

### The 3-3-3 Method
Study 3 hours → 30-minute gap → review 3 key points.

### Retrospective Timetable
At night, write what you actually studied instead of what you planned.

## 6. Make Yourself Dangerously Overeducated

1. **KAIZEN** — improve 1% daily, track progress in a notebook, review weekly.
2. **Strengthen fundamentals** — mental math, logic, reading comprehension, writing, conceptual understanding before memorization.
3. **Active learning** — teach others, do practice problems, summarize in your own words, quiz yourself.
4. **Cultivate discipline, not obsession.**
5. **Study deeply, not widely.**
6. **Develop mental endurance.**
