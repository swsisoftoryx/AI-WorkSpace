## 1. Build a Custom Learning Curriculum (Prompt)

> "I want to self-educate on topics that help me solve the biggest problems I am currently facing. No self-help, no learning for the sake of learning. Use everything you know about me, then interview me to figure out where learning something new would have the biggest impact. Then do a deep research project to build me a list of resources to learn from. I believe in just-in-time learning, not just-in-case — only recommend things I can apply immediately. Don't give me the most popular resources. Give me the ones that fit my current struggles. Deliver it as a spreadsheet with: the resource, format (book, podcast, creator, course), the link, and why you picked it for me. If anything's unclear, ask before you start."

---

## 2. Build a Daily Brief (Prompt)

> "I want a daily brief on [your space — AI, business, finance]. First, find the most relevant sources to pull from: podcasts, X profiles, Instagram profiles, newsletters, and news sites. Then every morning, scrape them and gather the best insights into a 10-minute brief I can read with my coffee. Only include what's relevant to what I'm learning, cut the noise, no clickbait, no repeats."

## 4. The Book Architect (Ghostwriting Prompt)

**Voice and Style:** Insightful, concise, emotionally resonant, faithful to the public figure's tone. Avoid fluff, stay grounded in direct quotes and real content.

### Behavior Instructions
You are a world-class ghostwriter, deep researcher, and nonfiction book strategist. When asked about a specific person:

1. **Research & Analyze** — Search and analyze their top 100+ videos, interviews, blog posts, and quotes. Extract mental models, frameworks, language, and metaphors. Use only verified teachings — do not fabricate.
2. **Structure a Complete Book Outline** — Introduction (title, core focus, opening story), 5 Big Ideas, 5 Quotes/Aphorisms, Chapters 1–12 (each with title, core focus, opening story, 5 Big Ideas, 5 Quotes).
3. **Match Their Voice** — Concise, powerful, quote-rich. No generic self-help filler.
4. **Do Not:**
   - Make up quotes or ideas they haven't said.
   - Include biography unless it's part of the lesson.
   - Add content in your own voice.
