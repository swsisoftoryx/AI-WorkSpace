# Comprehensive Financial & Investment Knowledge Base
## 1. Stock Valuation & Investment Strategies

### A. Magic Formula Investing
1. **Calculate Return on Capital Employed (ROCE):** $\text{ROCE} = \frac{\text{EBIT}}{\text{Capital Employed}}$
2. **Calculate Earnings Yield:** $\frac{\text{EBIT}}{\text{Enterprise Value (EV)}}$
3. **Rank Market Stocks:** Rank all available stocks on both metrics separately.
4. **Combine Ranks:** Add both rank numbers together. The stock with the lowest combined score wins.
5. **Execution:** Buy a basket of top-ranked stocks, hold for approximately 1 year, and repeat.

### B. Fair Value Calculation Frameworks

#### Strategy 1: Earnings & Dividend Yield Relative Valuation
$$\text{Result} = \frac{\text{TTM EPS Growth (\%)} + \text{Dividend Yield (\%)}}{\text{TTM P/E Ratio}}$$

* **Example (TCS):**
  * Current Price: ₹2,259
  * $A$ (TTM EPS Growth): $9\%$
  * $B$ (Dividend Yield): $2.82\%$
  * $C$ (TTM P/E Ratio): $15$
  * **Calculation:** $\frac{9 + 2.82}{15} = 0.788$

* **Valuation Rules:**
  * **Fairly Valued:** $1.0 < \text{Result} < 1.5$
  * **Overvalued:** $\text{Result} < 1.0$
  * **Undervalued:** $\text{Result} > 2.0$

#### Strategy 2: Sales-Growth Based Valuation
1. **Item 1:** Find current P/E ratio.
2. **Item 2:** Find TTM EPS (Earnings Per Share over last 12 months).
3. **Baseline Fair Price:** $\text{Item 1} \times \text{Item 2}$
4. **Item 3:** Find TTM Compounded Sales Growth % under Profit & Loss.
5. **Target Price Formula:**
   $$\text{Right Price} = \frac{\text{Item 1} \times \text{Item 2} \times \text{Item 3}}{100}$$
   * *Rule:* Once Item 3 (sales growth) increases, the fair price target moves upward accordingly.

## 2. Fundamental & Qualitative Analysis

### 1️⃣ Key Factors Before Investing
* **Business Fundamentals:**
  * **Business Model:** Understand revenue mechanics and competitive advantage (moat).
  * **Industry Position:** Determine if the company is a market leader or laggard.
  * **Management Quality:** Assess track record, integrity, and execution capability.
* **Financial Health:**
  * **Revenue Growth:** Consistent multi-year sales growth.
  * **Profitability:** Reliable earnings consistency.
  * **Debt Levels:** Manageable debt with strong interest coverage.
* **Competitive Advantage:** Brand strength, patents, cost advantages, customer retention.
* **Future Prospects:** Industry tailwinds (e.g., digitalization, clean energy) and active product innovation.

### 2️⃣ Important Financial Ratios Reference
| Ratio | Definition | Ideal Threshold |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **P/E Ratio** | $\frac{\text{Price}}{\text{EPS}}$ | Lower than industry peers; avoid excessive high multiples |
| **P/B Ratio** | $\frac{\text{Market Price}}{\text{Book Value}}$ | $< 3$ for long-term investments |
| **Debt to Equity** | $\frac{\text{Total Debt}}{\text{Equity}}$ | $< 1.0$ is preferred for conservative safety |
| **ROE** | $\frac{\text{Net Profit}}{\text{Shareholder Equity}}$ | $> 15\%$ indicates capital efficiency |
| **ROCE** | $\frac{\text{EBIT}}{\text{Equity} + \text{Debt}}$ | Higher is better; measures operational capital return |
| **Current Ratio** | $\frac{\text{Current Assets}}{\text{Current Liabilities}}$ | $> 1.0$ indicates short-term liquidity safety |
| **Dividend Yield** | $\frac{\text{Dividend}}{\text{Share Price}}$ | Sustainable yield for cashflow-focused portfolios |
| **Free Cash Flow** | $\text{Operating Cash} - \text{CapEx}$ | Positive; ensures cash for debt payoff, dividends, reinvestment |

### 3️⃣ Qualitative Checklist & Red Flags
* **Qualitative Checks:** Economic Moat, Management Integrity, Regulatory Exposure, Brand Strength.
* **Red Flags:**
  * Consecutive years of negative cash flow
  * High D/E ratio ($> 2$) without strong profits
  * Declining multi-year revenue or profit margins
  * Frequent pledging of promoter shares
  * Heavy insider selling

## 3. Financial Independence, Retire Early (FIRE) Strategies

| FIRE Strategy | Core Approach | Target Portfolio / Rule |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Traditional FIRE** | Maximum savings rate into low-cost index funds | Apply 4% Rule ($25\times$ annual living expenses) |
| **Entrepreneur FIRE** | Build, scale, and exit a high-margin business (E-com, SaaS, Agency) | Sell within 3–5 years or retain cash-flowing assets |
| **Coast FIRE** | Aggressive saving early until reaching a critical milestone | Stop adding capital; let compounding reach age 65 retirement |
| **Digital Nomad FIRE** | Remote income stream combined with a lower cost of living | Lower portfolio requirements via geographic arbitrage |
| **Extreme Frugality** | Minimize lifestyle expenses to absolute minimum threshold | Drastically reduces required portfolio target |
| **Hybrid FIRE** | Combine passive investment yield with partial active work income | Reduces reliance on pure drawdown calculations |
| **Dividend FIRE** | Build dividend-yielding stock basket to cover monthly expenses | Requires substantial equity capital (e.g., $1M+) |
| **Fat FIRE** | High-end retirement lifestyle support | Portfolio target typically range: $5M – $10M+ |


## 5. Trading Rules & Watchlists

### Trading Rules & Execution Mindset
* **Filter Trades:** Skip ~80% of setups; execute only on the top 20% highest-probability setups.
* **Managing FOMO / Hesitation:** If experiencing even 1% doubt, trade minimum lot size ($1 trade) to preserve psychology without emotional capital loss.
* **Visualization:** Use **Heikin Ashi** candlesticks for clearer assessment of support/resistance approaches.
* **Trailing Stop Loss:** Avoid selling great runners too early. Example: Bought at 100 $\rightarrow$ runs to 400 $\rightarrow$ place stop loss at 300 (25% trailing drawdown tolerance).
