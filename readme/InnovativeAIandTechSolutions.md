# Innovative AI and Tech Solutions

---

## **01. Burnout Recovery**
**What It Is:** A guided system to help individuals reset from screen fatigue and digital overload.
**Purpose:** Allows users to recover without abandoning digital life entirely.

---

## **02. AI Property Manager**
**What It Is:** An AI system that automates rent collection, repairs, and tenant inquiries for small landlords.
**Purpose:** Streamlines property management for efficiency and convenience.

---

## **03. Local Food Box**
**What It Is:** A weekly subscription box connecting consumers directly with local farms.
**Purpose:** Eliminates supermarket markups and supports local agriculture.

---

## **04. Sign Translator**
**What It Is:** A real-time translation tool between sign language and speech.
**Purpose:** Makes everyday conversations more accessible for the hearing-impaired community.

---

## **05. Senior Fraud Shield**
**What It Is:** An AI system that monitors transactions and detects suspicious activity.
**Purpose:** Protects elderly individuals from scams and financial fraud.

---

## **06. AI Code Reviewer**
**What It Is:** An AI-powered security layer that checks beginner and AI-generated code for vulnerabilities.
**Purpose:** Ensures code safety and reliability before deployment.

---

## **07. Remote Physio**
**What It Is:** A phone camera-based system that guides and corrects rehab exercises at home.
**Purpose:** Provides the experience of having a physical therapist monitor your progress remotely.

---
## **08. Battery Recycling**
**What It Is:** A pickup-and-recycling network for dead device and EV batteries.
**Purpose:** Addresses the growing issue of e-waste and promotes sustainability.

---
## **09. AI Meeting Doer**
**What It Is:** An AI tool that not only takes meeting notes but also converts decisions into actionable tasks.
**Purpose:** Ensures meeting outcomes are executed efficiently.

---
## **10. Simple Home Solar**
**What It Is:** A one-stop platform for designing, installing, and financing rooftop solar systems.
**Purpose:** Simplifies the process of adopting solar energy for homeowners.
